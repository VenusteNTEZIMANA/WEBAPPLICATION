About Us

Welcome to Lumina Hotel Kigali

Nestled in the beautiful hills of Kigali, Lumina Hotel is a premier 5-star luxury destination that combines modern elegance with warm Rwandan hospitality.

Since our opening, we have been dedicated to providing exceptional service, luxurious accommodations, and unforgettable experiences for our guests. With 120 elegantly designed rooms, multiple fine dining restaurants, a rooftop pool, and a world-class spa, Lumina Hotel offers everything you need for a perfect stay.
